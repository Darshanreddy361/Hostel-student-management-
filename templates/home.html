THE PLATFORM

Less chasing. More clarity.

Everyday hostel tasks are organized around the people responsible for them.

01 / ENROLLMENT

Campus-specific registration

Students create an account through the South or North Campus girls' hostel form using their USN and password.

02 / BEDS & ROOMS

Bed-aware allocation

Organize building, floor, room, and bed inventory; track occupancy and block beds that are unavailable.

03 / DAILY OPERATIONS

Attendance and complaints

Mark daily attendance, review student issues, and keep resolution status visible to both roles.

04 / FEE VERIFICATION

Private receipt review

Students upload receipts; wardens approve or reject with a recorded reason and verification history.

05 / MESS & MEALS

A menu for every meal

Publish breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner by campus and date; students see their hostel's daily menu.

06 / GATE WORKFLOW

Visitors and outpasses

Submit requests, record warden decisions, and log approved outpass exit and return events.

07 / CAMPUS CARE

Facilities and work orders

Report facility issues, set priority, assign campus staff, and document completed work.

08 / STAFF & COMMS

People, shifts, and notices

Maintain staff records, day/evening/night duty rosters, student notices, and hostel rules.

09 / OPERATIONS

Vendors and finance records

Keep categorized income, expense, vendor, invoice, and due/paid records in the warden workspace.

10 / REPORTING

Exportable registers

Download warden-only CSV reports for student, attendance, fee, gate, maintenance, and finance workflows.

11 / STUDENT SELF-SERVICE

One personal dashboard

Follow your room, meals, receipts, visitors, leave requests, maintenance tickets, notices, and contacts.

12 / ACCESS & PRIVACY

Role-scoped workspaces

Students access their own submissions while wardens manage authorized operations across both campuses.

BUILT-IN SAFEGUARDS

Practical protections for everyday use.

The portal uses standard safeguards in its account and form flows. It is a project demo, not a substitute for an institutional security review.

01
Hashed passwords

Passwords are stored as hashes rather than readable text.

02
Role-checked actions

Student and warden operations are restricted by account role.

03
Protected form submissions

State-changing forms include CSRF validation.

04
Clear integration boundaries

Fee review and finance entries are records only. Payment gateways, SMS/OTP, SIS/ERP, and access-control devices are not simulated as live services.
